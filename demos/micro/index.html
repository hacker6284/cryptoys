Microdemos

Each page loops one animation primitive from the real demo code, with its sounds. Tune timing and pick sounds by ear, then Copy settings for JSON shaped like the source demo's config. Settings persist per page; click once to turn sound on.

Twisty puzzles

  • Scramble face turn: lift, cubing.js turn(s), settle
  • Megaminx face turn: one, two or three clicks; recorded, composite or single-per-click

DoubleDeal cards

  • Deal into the grid (seatPacket)
  • Row slide and column belt (ShiftRows / SumRanks)
  • Scoop into the hand pile
  • Place a card (GridCycle place: hop or drop)
  • Take a card (inverse GridCycle)

Boxes and the toy chest

  • Unbox: flap, deck out, set down, sleeve aside, deal
  • Put back: cards in, flaps closed
  • MegaDreifach deck-box flap
  • Toy chest: lid, a toy flying out and back
