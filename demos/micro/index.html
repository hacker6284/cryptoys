Animation library, by object

One page per object move: one object, one move, one fixed placement, looping. ?seed=N picks another placement on load (carry varies its flights, since changing place is the move).

  • Deck: carry: the KEY and MSG deck boxes carried between poses on the felt and the shelf, a different flight every cycle (seeded: real poses, flips, half turns, shortest hop to longest flight); the chest stays shut and nothing else moves; each flight checked as drawn. deck/carry; not yet approved.
  • Deck: deal into the grid: the hand packet is dealt card by card into the message grid; real 63×88 mm cards, the two grids 8 columns × 13 rows. deck/deal; animation approved and locked at 7b5028f (sound on hold).
  • Deck box: open and close the flap: one tuck box in one placement opens its flap and closes it, looping; seeds 1–7 are table centre, shelf slot, lying on its back (felt stops it), against the shelf's backboard (backboard stops it), face down, on its side, at the felt edge; checked as drawn. deck/box; not yet approved.
  • Chest: open and close the lid: the toy chest, set down at one spot, opens its lid and lets it fall shut, looping; seeds 1–3 are its corner, its live spot (the wall stops the lid) and under the shelf (the shelf stops it); checked as drawn. chest; not yet approved.
  • Peg: insert, slide, remove: a white peg into a key-grid hole and out; a red peg into the lid's workspace, two rows along and out (the BS demo's peg moves). peg; not yet approved.
  • Ship: place, move, lift: a Destroyer laid on the key grid and swapped for the Sub as it grows; the walk cursor slides along the frame. ship; not yet approved.
  • Dice: roll: the BS key dice (d10, d12, d6) at real size, each thrown onto its next face. dice; not yet approved.
  • Cube: face turn: the cube lifts, turns R, settles; then R2 (double), then R3 (triple), each its own step with its own sound and landing pat. cube; animation and sounds approved and locked at 6014bfc (face turns); rotation approved 2026-10-02.
  • Cube: whole-cube rotation: the cube lifts, turns over as one piece (y), settles; the next loop turns it back. cube; rotation sound approved 2026-10-02; face turns locked at 6014bfc.
  • Megaminx: face turn: the megaminx lifts, turns U, settles; then U2 (double), then U3 (triple), each its own step with its own sound and landing pat. megaminx; animation and sounds approved and locked at a927bb2 (real 70 mm, seated on the felt).
