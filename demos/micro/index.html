Microdemos

Each page loops one animation from the playroom with its sound. Click once on a page to turn sound on.

  • Scramble face turn: the cube lifts, turns R, settles; the next loop turns it back. Library entry, approved: the playroom plays this.
  • Scramble whole-cube rotation: the cube lifts, turns over as one piece (y), settles; the next loop turns it back. Library entry (scramble-turn's rotation sound), approved: the playroom plays this.
  • Megaminx face turn: the megaminx lifts, turns one face one click, settles.
  • Deal into the grid: the hand packet is dealt into the 4×13 grid.
  • Row slide: a row of the grid slides three places along the felt and back.
  • Scoop: the whole grid is swept into the hand pile.
  • Place a card: a card hops from the hand pile to its seat in the grid.
  • Take a card: a seated card hops onto the hand pile.
  • Unbox: the deck box flap opens, the deck slides out and is set down.
  • Put the decks back: the grids gather onto both boxes and the flaps close.
  • MegaDreifach deck flap: the deck box flap opens for a deal and closes.
  • Toy chest: the lid opens, a deck flies out onto the table, the lid closes.
  • Peg: a peg is pushed into a grid hole and pulled out.
  • Dice cup: the cup shakes and pours dice into the felt tray.
