TwoDeck

Plaintext on the left. Key on the right.

You type text. TwoDeck encrypts a deck, which is one permutation of 52 cards. Section 5.3 of the specification is the conversion, and it sits outside encrypt and decrypt. A message block is 28 bytes. Ciphertext is 29 bytes per deck, because 52! does not fit in 28 bytes.
