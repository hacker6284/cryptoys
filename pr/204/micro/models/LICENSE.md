# Microdemo models

All five are **procedural blockouts made for this project** (modelled in Blender for cryptoys, 2026-09-30). They contain no third-party content and are project-owned; CC0 is intended. `ocean_grid_10x10_holes.glb` has one change from the original blockout: a deeper blue plate material (roughness 0.85), so the top reads as blue under the den's warm key light.

| File | Real size | Used by | Scrounge path |
|---|---|---|---|
| `peg_white.glb`, `peg_red.glb` | Ø 4.6 × 12.5 mm, origin at the tip | `peg/` | `models/procedural/` |
| `ocean_grid_10x10_holes.glb` | 147.6 × 147.6 × 3 mm, 100 × 3.2 mm holes at an estimated 13 mm pitch | `peg/` | `models/procedural/` |
| `proc_dice_cup_95mm.glb` | Ø 82 × 99.5 mm lathe cup | `dice-cup/` | `yahtzee/models/` |
| `proc_dice_tray_felt_260x180.glb` | 260 × 180 mm rimmed tray, felt inset | `dice-cup/` | `polyhedral/models/` |

The hole pitch and peg size are estimates (no caliper reading yet; see the scrounge MANIFEST §1).
